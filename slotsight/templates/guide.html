How to use SlotSight

SlotSight watches one parking lot through one camera and shows which bays are free, which are taken and which it can't tell. This page explains what you see on the dashboard and how to set it up for your lot.

This guide opens once, the first time you use SlotSight in this browser. Find it again under Guide in the dashboard's top bar.

Set up your lot

Five steps, in this order. Each one is on the dashboard.

  1. Install a detection model

    SlotSight downloads its default model the first time it runs. Until a model is ready the dashboard shows demo data and says so. To choose another model, open Settings, then Detection model and resolution, and press Install, then Use.

  2. Check a photo from your camera

    Under Camera and footage, drop a photo taken from your camera's position (or choose one) and press Check this file. The photo appears on the map with the vehicles it found boxed.

  3. Draw the bays

    Press Edit layout. Drag across a bay on the photo to draw it, then drag its corners to fit the painted lines. Each bay gets an id you can see on the map. Press Save layout when they all fit. Nothing changes until you save, and Undo takes back any step.

    Saving a changed layout also makes the current photo the camera reference, which SlotSight later compares against to warn you if the camera has moved.

  4. Connect the live camera

    In Live camera, enter the camera's stream address (for example rtsp://camera/stream), 0 for a webcam, or the path to a video file, and press Connect. SlotSight reads a frame every 2 seconds and updates the dashboard as it goes.

  5. Adjust if needed

    In Settings, Show as not sure below sets how confident SlotSight must be before it calls a bay free or taken. Raise it if you see wrong calls; more bays will then show as not sure. Every change is saved as you make it.

Read the dashboard

The bays

Free
Nothing is parked in the bay.
Taken
A vehicle is in the bay.
Not sure
The reading wasn't clear enough, for example in poor light or when something blocks the view. A bay that is not sure is never counted as free, so the free count errs on the safe side.

Point at a bay on the map, or move to it with the Tab key, to see how long it has had its status and how confident the reading is. A bay that just changed is outlined for a moment, and Recent changes lists every bay with the newest change first.

The status line

The top bar and the sign's lower line say what the numbers are based on.

Live
A camera is connected and readings are arriving.
Processing video
A video file is being read, a frame every few seconds of footage.
Last reading 14:32:08
The latest photo or video reading, from that time.
No new reading for 15 s
A camera is connected but has stopped sending frames. The map fades until readings return.
Camera disconnected, Camera lost
The camera was disconnected, or stopped working. The last reading stays on screen, faded.
Demo data
No detection model is installed yet, so the numbers are made up to show the layout. Nothing is recorded.
Model loading, Model error
The model is starting, or can't be used. Settings shows what to do.
Auto refresh off, Paused while editing
The dashboard has stopped asking for new readings until you switch it back on or finish editing.
Reconnecting
The dashboard can't reach SlotSight. Check that the app is still running.

Everyday use

  • Glance at the sign. The lit number is how many bays are free now. If it is grey, read the line beneath it first.
  • Watch for the camera warning. If the camera is knocked or turned, SlotSight says so above the map. Open Edit layout, fit the bays to the new view and save.
  • See the pattern. Over time shows free spaces through the last day or week and the busiest hours.
  • Take the history with you. Under History, download every reading as CSV or Excel: one row per bay per reading, with its status, confidence, source and time.
  • Choose light or dark. Settings, then Appearance. The choice is kept in this browser.

Check the accuracy

SlotSight's accuracy on your own lot hasn't been measured yet. It is measured by comparing its readings with frames you label by hand, in daylight and in the evening or rain.

  1. Record a few sessions of footage from the camera, and extract frames from them (see slotsight/docs/recording-guide.md).

  2. Open Label frames and mark each bay as taken or free, by eye.

  3. Run slotsight/eval/evaluate.py to compare SlotSight's readings with your labels.

Good to know

  • SlotSight is a prototype for one camera watching one lot.
  • It runs on this computer. Readings and history stay here; the only download is a detection model when you install one.
  • On a laptop without a graphics card, the dashboard updates every few seconds.
  • A reading that isn't confident enough, often in poor light or for far-away bays, is shown as not sure rather than guessed.
